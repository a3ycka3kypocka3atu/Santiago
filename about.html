How Lumeya works

Discovery with context

Lumeya is building an international network for finding holistic wellbeing services, practitioners, places, events and conscious-living approaches.

One connected relationship

People → Services → Practitioners → Places → Events → Relationships

Discover

Start from a need, service, person, place or topic instead of guessing the right category.

Understand

See scope, status, format, location and related work before choosing a contact action.

Locate and contact

Use honest location precision and direct contact. The MVP does not pretend to confirm bookings.

Curation and trust

A suggestion enters review; it does not become a public listing automatically. Unknown details stay unknown, event concepts stay separate from real dates, and city-level map positions are labelled as approximate.

Respectful scope

Lumeya is a discovery platform—not a healthcare provider. Listings are informational and do not replace qualified medical advice.

Minimal data

Public requests ask only for the context needed to reply. Do not submit medical records or sensitive health information.

Direct relationships

Contact actions connect people to a practitioner or organiser. Payments, social profiles and native booking are outside this MVP.

Shape the network

Recommend a useful listing or describe what you are trying to find. Both public flows work without an account and provide a Telegram fallback.

Open public forms

Earlier Santiago community and project pages remain available as historical context, but they are not part of the primary Public Discovery navigation.
